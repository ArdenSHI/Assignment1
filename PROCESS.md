# PROCESS.md — How I used AI on this essay

**Tools:** WorkBuddy (AI assistant). For this version I wrote the full outline myself in Chinese, with my own concrete examples (a VR-game brainstorm, a museum installation, a phone-case SKU-renaming tool disaster), and asked the model to produce a polished English version without changing the core argument.

**What I kept:** Everything substantive. All of my concrete examples made it in (the VR-game brainstorm and the blind test against my teacher and classmates; the project-management work on the VR game, a museum installation, and several e-commerce projects; the phone-case SKU tool catastrophe where the AI's renaming logic missed rare name formats and the script overwrote my original file with no backup). The three-stage structure and the closing argument — that the skill in question is independent thinking, and that learning fundamentals is how you build it — are mine, not the model's.

**What I rejected:** The model offered to fold more philosophical material into the conclusion (a passage on consciousness, free will, what "thinking" really means). I cut it: the point of the essay is practical — what to do differently in your own work — and the extra abstraction was diluting that. The model also proposed replacing one of my concrete examples with a more "elegant" hypothetical; the assignment explicitly says one real example examined properly beats three hypotheticals, so I kept all of them. The first English draft the model produced came in at 1,374 words; I trimmed it back to under 1,000 by removing redundant phrases, not by cutting content.

**On the earlier drafts:** The first version took a directing angle; the second centred on the describing/specifying gap. Both were built heavily from the model's suggestions. This third version is the one where I came in with the skeleton, the examples, and the conclusion already in my head, and asked the model to translate. The shift from mostly AI-argument-with-my-example to mostly-my-argument-with-AI-polish is, again, the essay's own point in miniature.
