# PROCESS.md — How I used AI on this essay

**Tools:** WorkBuddy (AI assistant) — heavily, and honestly, on the first draft: brainstorming the angle, building the structure, and drafting the full text. I also used its GitHub integration to push the first commits. The "make it lonelier" story is from a short I directed; the framing around it was built in conversation with the model.

**What I kept:** The framing of a film set as "a prompt-engineering workshop" — the observation that directing is already the discipline of converting felt intention into precise instructions. I'd been circling this idea from my own experience, and the model named the pattern cleanly. It became the spine of the essay because it turns my own practice into the argument instead of borrowing someone else's.

**What I rejected:** Two things. First, an opening the model produced about how "technology is changing faster than ever" — generic futurism, exactly what the assignment warns scores badly, and not my voice. Second, its suggestion to describe AI as "like hiring a very fast intern." An intern learns your taste; a model has its own defaults and will impose them every time you fail to be precise. The comparison sounded warm and was simply wrong — the counter-argument ended up in the essay instead (see "Defaults all the way down").

**What happens next:** This file describes the first-draft state. The revisions — cutting, tightening, and replacing anything that doesn't sound like me — are mine, and they arrive in the commits that follow.
